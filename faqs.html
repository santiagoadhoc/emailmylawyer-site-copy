Frequently Asked Questions

Talk With Us

  • Do you offer free consultations?

    Yes, we provide free consultations including bilingual support in English and Spanish. Marcus E. DeBose offers digital, in-person, or phone consultations that are always client-centered.

  • Are you available 24/7?

    Yes, we answer when you call day or night. Marcus provides 24/7 accessibility for emergency jail calls, warrant situations, and urgent legal matters.

  • Do you offer military discounts?

    Yes, we offer military discounts and compassionate defense for veterans and active-duty service members. We also coordinate with Veterans Court and VA representatives for specialized programs.

  • How long has Marcus practiced law?

    Marcus E. DeBose has been practicing criminal defense in San Diego since 2001. He's a graduate of University of San Diego School of Law with over 20 years of experience.

  • Can you help with arrest warrants?

    Yes, we offer discreet help with active warrants. Marcus can often arrange voluntary surrender or court appearances to avoid arrest while protecting your dignity and minimizing exposure.

  • Do you speak Spanish?

    Yes, Marcus E. DeBose is fully bilingual and fluent in English and Spanish. We provide bilingual consultations, forms, and clear legal explanations throughout your case.

  • What's your trial experience like?

    Marcus has litigated hundreds of felony cases with successful Not Guilty verdicts in felony armed robbery, elder abuse, and domestic violence trials in San Diego courts.

  • Can you reduce my bail?

    Yes, we represent clients seeking bail reduction or own recognizance release. Marcus advocates for fair terms emphasizing community ties and first-offender status at San Diego jails.

  • Do you handle insurance fraud cases?

    Yes, we defend individuals accused of filing false claims or exaggerating losses. Marcus works with investigators and prosecutors to negotiate dismissals or civil resolutions.

  • Can you clear my criminal record?

    Yes, we handle both expungements and record sealing. Marcus personally reviews eligibility and handles filings across San Diego County for employment and housing opportunities.

  • What areas do you serve?

    We serve San Diego, Los Angeles, Orange County, Riverside, Imperial County, and San Bernardino. Marcus has deep understanding of local courts, judges, and procedures.

  • How do you handle probation violations?

    We defend against probation violation accusations and negotiate for reinstatement or modification instead of jail. Marcus demonstrates credibility with San Diego probation officers and judges.
